## What's in it

**Mail** – import a list from Excel, review and edit every email, attach your CV once, send now or schedule at random times, tag people, read replies as conversations, answer inside the app and send follow-ups to those who did not reply.
**Resume maker** – 10 templates (including photo and personal-details layouts), live A4 preview, a Figma-style design mode, styling of selected words only, contact icons, bundled fonts and PDF download.
**You stay in control** – everything is stored in a folder you choose, with automatic backups, and the app handles a computer that was switched off in a safe, predictable way.

## Quick start

1. **Download** and start the app (see above).
2. **Settings → Email account**: pick your provider, enter your address and its password/code (the **How to connect** button explains each provider), save, press **Test connection**.
3. **Documents**: upload your CV and transcript (once).
4. **Import**: drop your Excel sheet, check the column mapping, tick the attachments, import.
5. **Emails**: look through the drafts, edit anything, tick the rows → **Schedule**.
6. **Check replies** and follow up with people who did not answer.
7. **Resumes**: build or edit a resume. It saves by itself.

New here? Press **How it works** on the Dashboard for a short illustrated tour.

## Mail guide

### 1. Connect your email account

Open **Settings → Email account**. The app sends your emails through **your own mailbox** using SMTP, so recipients see your real address and the messages also appear in your *Sent* folder.

> **Recommended in China: QQ Mail.** It is the simplest and most reliable way: it works without a VPN, delivers well to Chinese universities and companies, and only needs a free, one-time *authorization code*. Gmail and Outlook are often blocked or need a VPN there.

**The process, step by step (the same for every provider):**

1. **Pick your provider** from the tiles. The server address, port and encryption are filled in automatically.
2. Press **How to connect …** – a short guide opens with the exact steps and buttons that open the right page of your provider.
3. Create the **password the app needs** at your provider (an *authorization code* or *app password* – see the table). This is **not** your normal login password.
4. Back in the app, enter your **email address**, a **sender name** (shown to recipients), and paste the **code/password**. Optional: **Reply-to** sends replies to another address (for example your university mail).
5. Press **Save account**, then **Test connection**. You should see *"Connected to … and logged in as …"*.
6. Send yourself a test: open any email in **Emails** and press **Send test to me**.

![Email account](docs/screenshots/08-settings.png)

| Provider | What you need | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **QQ Mail** ⭐ | Authorization code | Recommended in China. See the detailed steps below. |
| **163 / 126 Mail** | Client authorization password | Switch on IMAP/SMTP in the mailbox settings first. |
| **Gmail** | 16-character **App Password** | Needs 2-Step Verification. Free Gmail allows about 500 recipients/day. Usually blocked in mainland China without a VPN. |
| **Yahoo Mail** | App password | Generate it in Yahoo account security. |
| **iCloud Mail** | App-specific password | Create it at appleid.apple.com (two-factor authentication required). |
| **Zoho Mail** | Password or app password | Change the server if your account is in another region (zoho.eu, zoho.in …). |
| **Microsoft 365 (work/school)** | Password / app password | Works only if your admin allows *Authenticated SMTP*. Microsoft is switching this off by default during 2026–2027. |
| **Outlook.com / Hotmail / Live** | – | **Limited:** Microsoft no longer accepts passwords or app passwords for SMTP on personal accounts (OAuth only). See the note below. |
| **Other (custom SMTP)** | Host, port, security, username, password | Any provider: university or company mail, web hosting, relay services. |

![Setup guide popup](docs/screenshots/14-setup-guide.png)

#### Detailed: QQ Mail (recommended)
1. Open <https://mail.qq.com> and sign in.
2. Click **Settings** (设置) → **Account** (账号与安全 / 账户).
3. Scroll to **POP3/IMAP/SMTP/Exchange/CardDAV/CalDAV service** and switch on **POP3/SMTP service** (or IMAP/SMTP).
4. QQ asks you to verify by **SMS**. Send the message it shows, then press the confirmation button.
5. QQ displays a **16-character authorization code** (授权码). **Copy it now** – it is shown once. If you lose it, generate a new one.
6. In the app: provider **QQ Mail**, email `yourname@qq.com` (or `12345678@qq.com`), paste the code into **Authorization code**, **Save account**, **Test connection**.

#### Detailed: Gmail
1. Open <https://myaccount.google.com/security> and turn on **2-Step Verification** (App passwords do not exist without it).
2. Open <https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords>, type a name such as *Outreach Mailer* and press **Create**.
3. Google may show the 16-character App Password in groups, such as `abtm qnib kood twnn`. Remove the spaces before entering it: `abtmqnibkoodtwnn`. The app also removes spaces automatically when saving.
4. In the app: provider **Gmail**, your full address, paste the app password, **Save account**, **Test connection**.
5. Stay under Gmail's daily limit: set **Daily send cap** in Settings (for example 100–200 for a first batch).

#### Detailed: 163 / 126 Mail
1. Sign in at <https://mail.163.com> (or <https://mail.126.com>) → **Settings** → **POP3/SMTP/IMAP**.
2. Switch on **IMAP/SMTP service** and confirm by SMS.
3. Create / copy the **client authorization password** (客户端授权密码).
4. In the app: provider **163 Mail** or **126 Mail**, your address, paste the authorization password, save and test.

#### Yahoo, iCloud, Zoho, Microsoft 365, custom SMTP
Choose the provider and press **How to connect …** – the popup lists the exact steps and opens the right page for you. For **custom SMTP** you only need the values your provider documents: server (host), port (**465** = SSL, **587** = STARTTLS), your email, and the password; fill **Username** only if it differs from your email address.

#### Why is Outlook.com marked "Limited"?
Microsoft switched off password sign-in (including app passwords) for SMTP on personal Outlook.com, Hotmail and Live accounts; only OAuth sign-in is accepted, which this app does not implement. Sending then fails with *"basic authentication is disabled"*. **Workaround:** send from another mailbox (QQ Mail, 163, Gmail …) and enter your Outlook address in **Reply-to** – replies still arrive in Outlook. Microsoft 365 work/school accounts may work only while your administrator allows password-based SMTP.

#### Common connection errors
| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| *Login rejected …* | Use the **authorization code / app password**, not your normal password. Make sure SMTP is switched on at your provider. |
| *Cannot reach the … server* | Check internet, firewall and VPN. Gmail / Microsoft servers may be blocked in mainland China – use QQ Mail instead. |
| *basic authentication is disabled* | Microsoft refuses password login for that account. Use another provider with Reply-to. |
| *daily sending limit* | Wait a day and lower the **Daily send cap** in Settings. |

### 2. Upload your documents

**Documents** → drop or choose files (CV, transcript, certificates; up to 10 MB each). They are stored once and can be attached to any batch or single email. Deleting a document also removes it from emails that used it.

### 3. Import your Excel sheet

**Import** → drop an `.xlsx`, `.xls` or `.csv` file. **The first row must contain the column headers.**

| Field | What to choose |
|---|---|
| **Batch name** | A label for this list, e.g. the university name. Used to filter later. |
| **Email address column** *(required)* | The column with the recipient address. |
| **Teacher name column** | Shown in the lists (optional). |
| **Skip rows already sent** | Pick a status column; rows whose value starts with *sent* or *replied* are skipped. |
| **Subject** | *Same for all* · *From a column* · *From the "Subject:" line in the draft* (that first line becomes the subject and is removed from the body). |
| **Email body** | *From a column* (ready-made drafts) or a *Template* with placeholders such as `{{Teacher name}}`, replaced from each row. |
| **Attachments** | Tick the documents attached to every email of this batch. |

**Preview** shows the first three emails exactly as they will be sent. Rows with an invalid address, an empty subject or an empty body are skipped and counted.

A typical sheet has the columns `Rank | Teacher name | School | Discipline | Sent status | Email | Draft email`. Common names are detected automatically; any other layout can be mapped by hand.

### 4. Review and edit emails

- **Status tabs** filter by status, the **search box** matches name, address and subject, the **batch** menu filters one import (and can delete the whole batch).
- Tick rows for **bulk actions**: Schedule, Unschedule, Attachments (replaces the attachments of all selected), Subject (replaces the subject), Delete.
- **Click a row** to edit it: recipient, subject, body, attachments · **Send test to me** (delivered to your own inbox with `[TEST]` in the subject) · **Send now** · **Mark replied**.
- Sent emails are locked, so history cannot be changed by accident.

### 5. Send or schedule

Select emails → **Schedule**.

| Mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Send now** | Queues the emails immediately. They go out **one at a time** with a short random pause (default 15–30 s), which is gentle on the mail server. About 15 minutes for 37 emails. |
| **Specific time** | All selected emails start at that date and time (still one at a time). |
| **Random in a date range** | Spreads the emails at **random times** between a start and an end. Optional: *daily time window* (e.g. 08:00–11:00), *skip weekends*, *minimum gap*, *shuffle order* (otherwise the top rows go first, so keep your best matches on top). **Preview times** shows the plan, **Re-roll times** draws a new one, **Confirm schedule** saves it. |

Times use the **time zone from Settings** (default `Asia/Shanghai`). **Unschedule** moves emails back to drafts at any time.

### 6. Statuses and what happens when the app is closed

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Imported, not scheduled. |
| **Scheduled** | Waiting for its time. |
| **Sending** | Being sent right now. |
| **Sent** | Accepted by your provider's mail server. |
| **Replied** | The person answered (found in your mailbox) or you marked it as answered. |
| **Failed** | Could not be sent. The reason is shown; fix it and schedule again. |
| **Missed** | Its planned time window passed while the app or PC was off. The app asks you once what to do, see [below](#8-if-the-app-or-the-computer-was-off). |

- **Closing the window keeps the app running in the system tray**, so scheduled emails still go out. Right-click the tray icon for *Open*, *Start when I sign in* and *Quit*.
- While emails are scheduled the app asks Windows not to suspend it.
- **Quit** warns you if emails are still scheduled.
- If the PC was off or asleep, see [what happens to scheduled emails](#8-if-the-app-or-the-computer-was-off).
- If the app is stopped *during* a send, that email is marked failed ("interrupted") and is **never re-sent automatically**. Check your mailbox's **Sent** folder first.

### 7. Tags, follow-ups and conversations

**Tags** – label people the way you would in a mail program: *Interested*, *Waiting*, *Meeting*, *Declined*, or anything you type.
- Select rows → **Tags** to add a tag to many emails (or remove one). Inside an email, type in the *Add tag…* box.
- Filter with the **All tags** menu, or search for a tag name.

![Conversation](docs/screenshots/15-conversation.png)

**Replies and conversations** – the app reads your mailbox over **IMAP** (same address and password as sending), finds the answers to your emails and shows them as a conversation, like in QQ Mail:
- Press **Check replies** on the Emails page. The app also checks by itself every 10 minutes while it runs (switch this off in Settings).
- Replies are matched to the email they answer (by the message headers, or by the sender's address). The status changes to **Replied**, a green badge shows the number of replies, and unread replies are highlighted. The Dashboard and a Windows notification tell you about new ones.
- Open an email → **Conversation** to see your first email, your follow-ups and their replies in order. Quoted old text is folded away. Type your answer and press **Send reply**: it goes out in the same thread (subject *Re: …*, proper `In-Reply-To` / `References` headers), so it stays grouped in their mail program too.
- Automatic replies (out of office) are shown but do not count as a real answer.
- **IMAP must be switched on at your provider.** In QQ Mail that is the same *IMAP/SMTP service* switch you use for sending. For a custom server you can enter the IMAP host and port in Settings → Email account.

**Follow-ups** – nobody answered?
- **Follow-up due** – a sent email without a reply gets an amber **Follow up** badge after **7 days** (change it in Settings). The quick filter **Follow-up due** and the Dashboard show them all.
- Select them → **Follow up** → write one message (use `{{name}}`), optionally quote your original email → the app creates one **reply-in-thread draft** per person (*Re: …*, tagged *Follow-up*). Review them, then **Schedule** like any other email. Follow-ups are never created for people who already answered.
- **No follow-up needed** (inside the email) switches the reminder off for one person.

![Follow-up](docs/screenshots/16-followup.png)

### 8. If the app or the computer was off

Every scheduled email remembers its own **time window**: the end of its daily window (for example 9:00), or the end of the date range. For *send now* and *specific time* the window is the planned time plus the grace period from Settings (60 min).

| When the app starts again | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Before** the window (e.g. you switch on at 7:00 for an 8:00–9:00 schedule) | Nothing special. Emails go out at their planned times. |
| **Inside** the window (e.g. 8:30) | Emails whose time already passed are sent **right away**, one after another, and the rest follow their schedule. **You are not asked.** |
| **After** the window (e.g. 10:00) | Nothing is sent. The emails become **Missed** and you get **one** question for **all** of them: **Send all now**, **Choose a new time…** (the normal schedule dialog) or **Keep as drafts**. |

![Missed emails](docs/screenshots/18-missed.png)

### 9. Live view, counts and filters
- The email list **refreshes by itself every few seconds**: when an email changes from *Scheduled* to *Sending* to *Sent*, or a reply arrives, you see it without reloading.
- **Showing 12 of 40 emails** is always visible, and the numbers on the status tabs follow your batch, tag and search filters. **Clear filters** resets everything.
- Quick filters: **Follow-up due**, **Unread replies**, **No reply yet**.

### 10. Dashboard and the guided tour
The Dashboard shows a **Getting started** checklist (connect email → upload documents → import → schedule), alerts for unread replies, follow-ups and missed emails, and a **How it works** button that opens a colourful step-by-step tour of the whole app. The tour opens by itself the first time.

![Guided tour](docs/screenshots/17-tour.png)

### Settings reference

| Setting | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Pause all sending | off | Stops the queue instantly. |
| Daily send cap | 0 (no limit) | Maximum emails actually sent per day. |
| Time zone | Asia/Shanghai | Used for schedules and the daily cap. |
| Min / max pause between emails | 15 / 30 s | A random value in this range after every email. |
| Grace after a planned time | 60 min | For *send now* / *specific time*: how late the app may still send after it starts again. After that the email is *Missed*. |
| Follow-up reminder after | 7 days | Sent emails without a reply get a **Follow up** badge after this time. |
| Look for replies | on | Checks your mailbox every 10 minutes (needs IMAP). |
| Theme | Dark | Dark / Light / Auto. The resume editor follows it. |

## Resume guide

Open **Resumes** in the sidebar.

- **My resumes** – thumbnails, **Edit**, **Copy**, **Delete**, search. The path of your Resume folder is shown at the bottom with an **Open folder** button.
- **Templates** – the six formats, each as **Use with sample** or **Start blank**.
- **Guide** – formatting tips, the JSON format, a copy-paste **AI prompt** and an FAQ.
- Top buttons: **New resume**, **From sample**, **Import JSON** (paste a resume as JSON, e.g. produced by an AI assistant from your old CV).

### The editor
- **Left:** form for *Style & layout* (font size, spacing, margins, font, accent colour), *Basics* and every section. Add and reorder sections, entries and bullets.
- **Right:** live, paginated **A4 preview** that matches the PDF.
- **Top bar:** back, undo/redo (`Ctrl+Z` / `Ctrl+Shift+Z`), **Design** mode, resume name (rename any time; the file is renamed too), template switcher, saved indicator, theme toggle, **JSON** (export/import), **PDF file**, **Download PDF** and a **Keyboard shortcuts** button.
- **Text formatting:** `**bold**`, `[link text](https://url)`, one line = one bullet.
- **Zoom:** `Ctrl` + `+` / `-`, `Ctrl 0` fit to width, `Ctrl 1` 100%.

### Design mode
Click **Design** to place free elements: **Move (V)**, **Hand (H)**, **Rectangle (R)**, **Ellipse (O)**, **Line (L)**, **Text (T)**, images and the ⭐ menu with **profile photo**, **skill bar** and **icons**. Drag to move, use the handles to resize, right-click for the context menu.

### Style only the words you select
In design mode double-click a text, **select some words** and a small toolbar appears above them: **bold, italic, underline, bigger / smaller, text colour** and *Tx* (clear). Only the selected words change, not the whole paragraph. The styling is stored inside the text (for example `{{c=#2563eb b|selected words}}`) so it also works in the form, in the JSON and in the PDF. The inspector on the right still styles the **whole element** when you select it on the canvas.

![Selection styling](docs/screenshots/19-inline-style.png)

### Bullets move with their text
When you drag a bullet point in design mode its bullet now moves together with the text (it used to stay behind).

### Spacing
- **Section spacing** sets the default gap between sections.
- **Space below header** adds space between the header block and the first section.
- Every section also has its own **Space above** slider (with **Auto** to go back to the default), so you can open up exactly one gap.

### Contact icons
Turn on **Show icons next to email, phone, location & links**. Each detail gets an icon chosen automatically (envelope, phone, pin, and LinkedIn, GitHub, X, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Telegram, WhatsApp, WeChat, QQ, ResearchGate, ORCID, Google Scholar … detected from the link). The icon belongs to its text, so they **move together** in design mode. Under *Contact icons* you can change or remove the icon of any single item.

### Photo, gender, age and other personal details

They appear in the four templates marked *(photo)*: **Photo · Classic**, **Photo · Sidebar**, **Photo · Header band** and **Info grid · 中文简历** (personal-info grid with a portrait photo, in the style of Chinese CVs). Photo shape (circle / rounded / square) and size are adjustable.

## Folders, backup and restore

You control three locations.

| What | How you choose it | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Install folder** (the program) | In the **installer** (*Choose install location*), or unzip/run the portable file from anywhere | Your user programs folder |
| **Data folder** (emails, documents, settings and the `Resume` folder) | **Settings → Storage & backup → Change folder…** | `%APPDATA%\Outreach Mailer\data` |
| **Backup folder** | **Settings → Storage & backup → Choose…** | none until you choose one |

### Change the data folder
**Change folder…** → pick a folder, for example `D:\OutreachMailer`, or one inside **OneDrive / Dropbox** for an automatic off-site copy.
- If it is empty you are asked to **move** your data there: `db.json` and the whole `Resume` folder are copied, then the app **restarts** with the new location.
- If it already contains Outreach Mailer data (for example from another PC) choose **Use the data in that folder**. Your old data stays untouched.
- If the folder is unavailable later (an unplugged drive) the app tells you and falls back to the default location.

### Back up
Choose a backup folder, then **Back up now** – or leave it: the app also backs up **automatically about once a day** and keeps the **newest 30** copies. One backup is one file, `outreach-backup-YYYY-MM-DD-HH-MM.json`, holding emails, documents, settings **and all resumes**.

### Restore
**Restore…** → choose a backup file → confirm. The app restarts with that data. Your previous data is kept as `db.before-restore-….json` and `Resume.before-restore-…` in the data folder, so a restore can be undone.

### New computer
Copy the data folder (or a backup file) over, install the app, then **Change folder… → Use the data in that folder** or **Restore…**.

> **Keep backups private.** Your email password / authorization code is stored in plain text in the data folder and in backups so that sending works. Never upload them to a public place.

## Publishing a release (for the maintainer)

`.exe` files are far too large to commit to the repository (GitHub's file limit there is 100 MB), but **GitHub Releases accept files up to 2 GB** and are the right place. The download buttons above point to the latest release, so the file names must stay exactly `Outreach-Mailer-Setup.exe` and `Outreach-Mailer-Portable.exe`.

**Automatic (recommended)** – GitHub builds the installer for you:
```bash
git tag v1.0.0
git push origin v1.0.0
```
The workflow in `.github/workflows/build-desktop.yml` runs on a Windows machine (about 5–8 minutes), builds both files and creates the release with the files attached. Check progress in the **Actions** tab.

**Manual** – build locally with `npm run dist:win`, then **Releases → Draft a new release**, choose a tag (e.g. `v1.0.0`), drag in the two files from `release/`, and publish.

For a new version: raise `"version"` in `package.json`, commit, push, then tag again (`v1.0.1`, …).

| `DATA_DIR` | Where `db.json` and the `Resume` folder live (default `./data`). |
| `PORT` | HTTP port (default 3000). |

## Troubleshooting & FAQ

**"Cannot reach smtp.qq.com:465"** – check your internet, firewall or VPN. Some VPNs and company networks block SMTP.

**"Login failed" on Test connection** – use the **SMTP authorization code**, not your QQ password, and make sure SMTP is switched on in QQ Mail. Generate a new code if unsure.

**Replies do not show up.** IMAP must be enabled at your provider (QQ / 163 / 126: switch on *IMAP/SMTP service*) and you must use the same authorization code / app password. Press **Check replies** to see the exact error. Gmail also needs the App Password; Outlook.com personal accounts cannot be read with a password.

**The app icon shows Electron.** Use the installer or portable file from the Releases page: the app icon and name are embedded in them. If Windows still shows an old icon after updating, restart Explorer or clear the icon cache (delete `%LOCALAPPDATA%\IconCache.db`), then unpin and re-pin the shortcut.

**Will my emails land in spam?** Nobody can promise inbox delivery. Send a test to another address first, send in a few batches, keep the message short and personal, and follow up after about a week. The app sends plain-text mail one by one, with natural pauses and proper `Date` / `Message-ID` headers.

**Do I need to keep the app running?** Yes, scheduled emails are sent by the app on your PC. Closing the window is fine (it stays in the tray). Quitting, shutting down or sleeping stops sending, and missed emails are flagged so you can reschedule them.

**An email shows "Failed – interrupted".** The app was closed during exactly that send. Look in your mailbox's **Sent** folder; if it is not there, schedule it again.

**Where is my data?** The path is shown in **Settings → Storage & backup**. Changing the folder never deletes the old one.

**Is my data sent anywhere?** No, see below.

## Privacy and security

- All data stays in your data folder on your computer. The desktop app has no accounts, analytics or cloud services.
- Your email password / authorization code is stored **unencrypted** in the data folder and in backups. Treat them like a password file.
- The desktop app has no login page; its built-in server only listens on `127.0.0.1`.
- Links (GitHub, LinkedIn) open in your default browser.
- Please send honest, relevant and personal messages, and respect the rules of your school or employer when emailing people you do not know.
